### [Fusion](https://pamaral-git.github.io/fusion/) ◦ Interactive equity research report
- Reusable, offline-first research template: Bear/Base/Bull scenarios drive the operating model, valuation, charts and a research brief together, all recalculating live in the browser.
- Valuation engine blends EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA and DCF into a risk-adjusted target, with a sensitivity matrix and a per-share price bridge.
- Evidence ledger separates primary-source claims from assumptions, and exports an AI research brief (copy or .md download) so a local model can continue the work without losing the audit trail.
